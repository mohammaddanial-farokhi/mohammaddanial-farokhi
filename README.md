## 👋 About Me

I'm a machine learning engineer specializing in the efficient adaptation of large language models.<br>
My focus is **parameter-efficient fine-tuning** (LoRA, QLoRA, prompt tuning) and **preference alignment** with DPO.<br>
My work takes open-weight language models and shapes them into focused tools: adapted to a specific task, evaluated against measurable criteria, and delivered without excess.
